### Privacy Policy

**Effective date**: [EFFECTIVE_DATE]

#### 1. Who we are

Zenith Care Solutions LLC ("**Zenith**", "**we**", "**us**", or "**our**") is a Wyoming limited liability company providing Chronic Care Management and related clinical support services to U.S. healthcare providers. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information collected through our website at **https://zenithcaresolutions.com** (the "**Site**").

This policy covers only the Site. It does **not** cover the protected health information (PHI) we handle under separate Business Associate Agreements with client practices — that information is governed by HIPAA, HITECH, our BAA with each client, and our internal privacy and security safeguards. **No PHI is collected or stored through this Site.**

#### 2. Information we collect

We collect only the information you voluntarily provide, plus a minimal set of technical information collected automatically.

**Information you provide**:
- Contact form submissions: your name, business email, practice name, U.S. state, role, and the message you send.
- Chatbot interactions: your clicks on scripted options. The chatbot does not accept free-text input and does not transmit anything beyond the scripted navigation state.
- Communications you initiate directly with us by email, WhatsApp, or phone.

**Information collected automatically**:
- Pageview analytics (pages visited, referrer, country, device type) via a privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics tool ([Plausible or Umami]).
- Server-side request logs retained by our hosting provider (Vercel) for a limited period for security and operational purposes.

**We do not use cookies for tracking.** We do not operate ad networks, retargeting pixels, or third-party trackers on the Site.

#### 3. How we use information

We use the information you provide to:
- Respond to your inquiry and schedule a discussion
- Prepare a service proposal and contract if you request one
- Maintain records of our business communications

We use automatic information to:
- Measure aggregate Site traffic and performance
- Diagnose errors and improve the Site

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your information to third parties for marketing purposes.

#### 4. Service providers we rely on

We use the following third-party services to operate the Site. Each is bound by its own privacy policy, and we only share with them the information required for their function.

| Provider | Function | Data shared |
|---|---|---|
| Vercel, Inc. | Website hosting | Standard web request logs |
| Formspree, Inc. | Contact form processing | Form submission contents |
| Calendly LLC | Discovery call scheduling | Booking details you enter in Calendly |
| [Plausible / Umami] | Website analytics | Aggregate pageview data; no personal identifiers |
| Google Workspace / Zoho | Email we send and receive | Email content |

If a contact form submission or email contains health information you provided (which we ask you not to send), that information is handled under the service provider's own terms. **Please do not send patient-identifying information through the Site or by email.** Use the secure channels we provide once a BAA is in place.

#### 5. How long we keep information

- Contact form submissions and email correspondence: retained for the duration of the business relationship and for up to [RETENTION_PERIOD, e.g., 3 years] thereafter for recordkeeping.
- Analytics data: aggregate only; retained indefinitely for trend analysis. No personal identifiers are stored.

#### 6. Security

We maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect the information we collect. The Site is served over HTTPS. We restrict access to submitted information to personnel with a business need. **No method of internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure.** You use the Site at your own risk and should not transmit sensitive information through it.

#### 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:
- Request access to the personal information we hold about you
- Request correction or deletion of your personal information
- Opt out of certain uses of your information
- Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

Specifically:
- **California residents (CCPA/CPRA)**: you have the right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA.
- **Virginia residents (VCDPA)**: you have the right to access, correct, delete, and port your personal information, and to opt out of targeted advertising, sale, or certain profiling.
- **Colorado residents (CPA)**: similar rights to the VCDPA.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at **[PRIVACY_CONTACT_EMAIL]**. We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law.

#### 8. Children

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

#### 9. International users

Zenith serves U.S. healthcare providers. The Site is hosted in the United States. If you access the Site from outside the U.S., you understand that your information will be transferred to and processed in the U.S.

#### 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be indicated by updating the "Last updated" date and, where appropriate, posting a notice on the Site.

### HIPAA Statement

**Last updated**: [LAST_UPDATED_DATE]

#### Our role under HIPAA

Zenith Care Solutions LLC provides Chronic Care Management ("CCM") and related clinical support services to U.S. healthcare providers under contract. When we handle protected health information (PHI) on behalf of a client practice, we operate as a **Business Associate** under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA), as amended by the HITECH Act, and the regulations issued thereunder (collectively, "HIPAA").

#### This website collects no PHI

This website (**https://zenithcaresolutions.com**) is a marketing and informational resource for prospective client practices. **It does not collect, store, process, or transmit protected health information.** Our contact forms and chatbot are limited to business-contact information from providers and administrators. We ask every visitor **not** to submit any patient-identifying information through this Site or by unencrypted email.

#### Our Business Associate Agreements

Before Zenith receives or handles any PHI on behalf of a client practice, we sign a **Business Associate Agreement** (BAA) with that practice. The BAA governs:
- Permitted uses and disclosures of PHI
- Safeguards we must maintain
- Breach notification obligations
- Return or destruction of PHI at the end of the engagement

Our BAA is based on current HHS sample provisions and may be reviewed by prospective clients during the contracting process.

#### Safeguards we maintain

**Administrative**: workforce training on HIPAA and the Minimum Necessary rule; written policies and procedures; designated privacy and security contact; sanctions for workforce violations; access reviews.

**Physical**: locked workspaces for personnel handling PHI; device inventory; secure disposal of hardware.

**Technical**: encrypted transmission of all PHI; access via client-controlled EHR/CCM platforms rather than exported data; multi-factor authentication; audit logging; secure password management; encrypted email tooling (HIPAA-eligible Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, each with a signed BAA from the respective vendor).

#### Minimum Necessary

Zenith accesses, uses, and discloses only the Minimum Necessary PHI to perform the services required under each client contract.

#### Breach notification

In the event of a breach of unsecured PHI, Zenith will notify the affected client practice without unreasonable delay and in no case later than the timeframe required by HIPAA and the governing BAA. The client practice remains responsible for notifying affected individuals and HHS as required by law.

#### What this statement is not

This statement describes Zenith's operational posture. It is **not** a Notice of Privacy Practices (which is a document covered entities provide to their patients), and it is **not** a substitute for a signed Business Associate Agreement. Prospective clients should request a BAA before any PHI is shared.

### Accessibility Statement

**Last updated**: [LAST_UPDATED_DATE]

#### Our commitment

Zenith Care Solutions is committed to making its website accessible to the broadest possible audience, including people with disabilities. We work to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA.

#### What we do

- Semantic HTML and clear document structure
- Keyboard navigation across all interactive controls
- Visible focus indicators
- Sufficient color contrast for text and interactive elements
- Descriptive alternative text for meaningful images
- Form fields with associated labels and error descriptions
- Scripted chatbot built with real `<button>` elements and keyboard support
- Respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`

#### Ongoing effort

Accessibility is a continuing process. We test with automated tools (Lighthouse, axe) and periodic manual review (keyboard-only navigation, screen-reader smoke tests). If you encounter a barrier, we want to know.

#### Report an issue

Please email **[ACCESSIBILITY_CONTACT_EMAIL]** with:
- The page URL
- A description of the barrier you encountered
- The assistive technology, browser, and operating system you were using (if any)

We aim to acknowledge accessibility reports within 2 business days and to address substantive issues within 15 business days when feasible.

#### Third-party content

Some of our pages embed third-party services (Calendly for scheduling, Formspree for form delivery). Those providers manage their own accessibility; we have selected tools that publish accessibility statements and conform to WCAG where possible, but we cannot guarantee the behavior of third-party interfaces.
