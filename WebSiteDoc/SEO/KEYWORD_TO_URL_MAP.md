## 3. Potential Keyword Cannibalization Issues

| Risk | Pages | Assessment | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| "chronic care management services" | Homepage vs `/services/chronic-care-management` | Low risk: homepage targets "chronic care management company" (brand/entity); service page targets "chronic care management services" (specific service query) | Monitor in GSC. If both pages compete, add `canonical` or consolidate homepage CCM copy into a brief mention with link. |
| "care management services" | `/services/care-management` (category hub) vs individual service pages (CCM, PCM, etc.) | Low risk: category hub targets the umbrella term; individual pages target specific programs | Ensure category hub links prominently to all child pages. Do not optimize category hub for specific program keywords. |
| "revenue cycle management" | `/services/revenue-cycle-management` (category) vs `/services/medical-billing` et al. | Low risk: same hierarchy pattern as care management | Keep RCM category as overview; child pages target specific services |
