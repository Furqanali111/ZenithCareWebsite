## Content Principles

1. **Quality over quantity.** One well-researched, clinically reviewed article per week beats three thin ones.
2. **Every article must link to at least one service page** and one other article.
3. **Every article needs clinical/billing review** before publishing. Do not publish unsupported reimbursement claims, eligibility criteria, or compliance guidance without qualified review.
4. **No fabricated data.** Use "representative", "typical", or cite specific CMS sources. Do not invent statistics, patient outcomes, or customer results.
5. **AI-friendly structure:** Question-based H2s, self-contained paragraphs, comparison tables, cited sources.
